# Purpose and independent directions — v0.1.2

The revised workflow normally opens with one question to establish the purpose, intention, and value to preserve. It then derives different ways to deliver that value, compares their benefits and burdens, and only afterward locates the current arrangement and considers transition work. A purpose already settled in the current exchange carries forward; an explicit proposal-first request uses conditional assumptions where needed.

Five final-version next-response samples met their focused criteria. One scripted continuation checked a correction to the intended value followed by a request for proposals. These are synthetic conversations, not private user transcripts or evidence of real-user usefulness.

## Correction and continuation

After the purpose-confirmation response was saved, the same actor received a scripted correction: the value to preserve was independent judgment in unexpected situations, with a request to compare proposals next. The [next response](responses/final-purpose-corrected.md) accepted that settled purpose without asking again. It derived decision references, unfamiliar-case practice, and supported workplace decisions, then located the existing materials and sessions conditionally. It treated teaching effort as an implementation burden rather than retaining it as the prior goal.

The actor was not shown the correction before its opening response. This checks one two-response path with an explicit transition cue; it is not a complete conversation or a test of whether the model would choose the right moment to propose without that cue.

## Comparison evidence

The [no-skill control](responses/control-no-skill-custom-documentation.md) already recommended dropping custom Docs management and gave useful tradeoffs. Its third option reproduced the current taxonomy and ledgers inside the option set, instead of locating the current arrangement separately afterward. The [v0.1.1 sample](responses/baseline-custom-documentation.md) also offered a substantial reset, but its comparison began with a partial change to the current setup, followed by a minimal entrance and no dedicated management.

These comparisons do not support a claim that the previous skill or ordinary dialogue could not consider radical alternatives. They illustrate the newly requested generation and comparison order. The final documentation sample offers two options; other samples offer three. That is observed flexibility in this small set, not a guarantee of freedom from anchoring.

## Setup and limits

Final actors and the no-skill control inherited the calling session's model and reasoning effort without a per-agent override. The earlier v0.1.1 comparison requested `gpt-6-astra` with `medium` reasoning. The exact backend build and effective configuration were not independently exposed. Because the recorded selection methods differ, this is not a matched estimate of model-controlled improvement.

Each actor received only its synthetic case, supplied source context where present, and the assigned skill snapshot. Scoring criteria, worked examples, and other responses were withheld. The primary Codex agent reviewed all eight saved responses semantically. [report.json](report.json) records the source hashes, setup, and criterion-level judgments.

There is one sample per case and arm, without repeated sampling of the same case. No statistical improvement, complete paired dialogue, human-perceived insight, native question UI, global installation flow, or Windows/Linux behavior is established. Historical tests and intermediate drafts do not count as final-version evidence. The broader conversation evaluation remains open in [issue #1](https://github.com/NemuKei/zero-base-thinking/issues/1).
